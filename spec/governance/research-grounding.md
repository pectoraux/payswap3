# Research Grounding

The implementation process is intentionally informed by external research, but research is not an authority over the repository.

Relevant bodies of work include:

- financial-market-infrastructure principles for legal basis, risk management, settlement finality, safeguarding/segregation and operational resilience;
- CPMI interoperability and harmonised payment-message work;
- liquidity-saving mechanisms and queue/netting research;
- digital-twin and simulation research on synchronization, model fidelity and counterfactuals;
- platform ecosystem research on modularity, complementors and incentive design;
- AI assurance research emphasizing testing, validation, monitoring and uncertainty.

The Architect records material research-driven architectural changes through ACRs; workers do not silently change the constitution because a paper suggests another design.

# Architecture Research Rationale

The architecture is informed by payment interoperability and financial-market-infrastructure research, including:

- CPMI/ISO 20022 harmonisation work for structured cross-border payment data and exceptions/investigations;
- BIS Nexus interoperability and account/proxy resolution;
- BIS Agorá on programmable multi-currency settlement and differentiated monetary claims;
- PFMI principles on legal basis, settlement finality, risk, segregation/portability, operational resilience and orderly wind-down;
- liquidity-saving mechanism literature on queuing, netting, auctions and settlement-delay trade-offs;
- digital-twin and simulation literature on model fidelity, synchronization, counterfactual reasoning and feedback;
- platform ecosystem research on composability, complementor quality and incentives.

The research does not become authority. Normative architecture is frozen in `spec/architecture/v0.1/` and changed only through an ACR.
